What triggers the animations?

OrbitalMark already runs ambient loops. NsibidiGlyphs are static. The uli-rule dividers are static. Which trigger model should the new animations use?

A

Scroll-triggered — elements animate once as they enter the viewport

Glyphs draw themselves when you scroll to them. Uli dividers extend left-to-right on entry. Section content fades up. Feels like the page is being revealed as you read down. Most impactful on the homepage.

B

Ambient loops — elements animate continuously in the background

Glyphs pulse with a slow heartbeat. Geometric patterns rotate or breathe. Works alongside OrbitalMark's existing loop. Feels alive and cosmic at all times.

C

Both — scroll reveals the element, then it loops ambientally

On scroll enter: glyph draws itself (ceremonial, gold). After it lands: slow ambient pulse begins (teal for system elements). Most layered, most work, most alive.
