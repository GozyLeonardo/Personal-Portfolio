Design Philosophy for this Redesign

The geometry is not decoration. Igbo-Ukwu interlacing circles, Uli curves, Nsibidi glyphs — every shape carries a function. Sections transition using geometry, not empty space.

90% black · 8% gold · 2% teal. Solar gold marks human/founder moments. Electric teal marks system/infrastructure moments. Warm off-white carries the narrative. Never invent new colours.

Cosmic = scale without noise. Large negative space is intentional. The geometry has weight because the space has weight. Nothing decorative. Every mark earns its place.

Each section deepens the cosmology. Hero = the cosmos origin point. About = the ancestral grounding. Projects = proof of chi at work. Skills = the tools of return. Empire = the long arc. Contact = the invitation to the line.
