# COSMIC AFRICAN SOUL — Designer's Manifesto

**Origin:** Lawrence Nwuzor | May 7, 2026  
**Purpose:** Personal design philosophy and visual system

## The Five Principles
1. **Inheritance of Darkness** — Foundation Black as primordial material
2. **Geometry of Memory** — Circles (Igbo-Ukwu), Curves (Uli), Glyphs (Nsibidi)
3. **Discipline of Light** — Solar Gold, Electric Teal, Warm Off-White cosmology
4. **Weight of Type** — Typography as architecture across four tiers
5. **Refusal of Decoration** — Structural only, no fill elements

## Brand Colours (for personal site)
- #080808 Foundation Black
- #C47A00 Solar Gold

- #F2EDE4 Warm Off-White

## Typographic Hierarchy
- Ojuju Bold → Display/Hero
- Space Grotesk → Headlines
- Inter → Body
- IBM Plex Mono → Technical
- Akagu (Neo-Nsibidi) → Iconographic

**Philosophy:** Africanfuturist aesthetic as a third way — not African decoration on Western minimalism, but African visual grammar as native structural elements.
